# Google Play Store Publishing Guide

This guide details the step-by-step procedure to publish **Talli** to the Google Play Store.

---

## Step 1: Create a Google Play Developer Account
1. Visit the [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console/signup).
2. Sign in with a Google account.
3. Choose your account type: **Personal** (20 testers required for 14 days before publishing) or **Organization** (can publish directly).
4. Complete the registration and pay the one-time **$25 USD** developer fee.
5. Verify your identity with a valid ID (passport or driver's license).

---

## Step 2: Create Your App in Play Console
1. Open the Play Console dashboard.
2. Click **Create App** in the top right.
3. Fill in the basic info:
   - **App Name**: `Talli Scorekeeper`
   - **Default Language**: English (United States)
   - **App or Game**: App
   - **Free or Paid**: Free
4. Accept the developer declarations and click **Create App**.

---

## Step 3: Complete App Declarations
In the left sidebar, scroll down to **Grow** -> **Store presence** -> **App content**.
Complete all required declarations (takes about 10 minutes):
- **Privacy Policy**: Provide a link to a privacy policy (you can host a simple markdown file on GitHub Gist or use a free generator).
- **Ads**: Declare that your app contains **no ads**.
- **App Access**: Choose "All functionality is available without special access."
- **Content Rating**: Complete the questionnaire. Select the category **Utility/Utility-Alternative** or **Entertainment** to receive an "All Ages" (3+) rating.
- **Target Audience**: Select **13 and older** or **Everyone** (if everyone, ensure compliance with COPPA).
- **News Apps**: Declare that your app is **not a news app**.
- **COVID-19 Contact Tracing**: Select "My app is not a publicly available COVID-19 contact tracing or status app."
- **Data Safety**: Declare that your app collects **no user data** (since everything is stored locally on-device).

---

## Step 4: Set Up Store Listing & Visual Assets
Navigate to **Grow** -> **Store presence** -> **Main store listing**.
Fill out the listing details:
- **Short Description**: `The ultimate premium, offline-first scoreboard & tally counter.`
- **Full Description**: 
  ```text
  Talli is an ultra-premium, beautiful scorekeeping application made for board games, card games, tally counts, and 1v1 sports matches. 

  Featuring a gorgeous dark glassmorphic design, custom vibrant color schemes, satisfying retro synthesizer sound effects, and built-in Text-To-Speech (TTS) voice announcements, Talli elevates game nights.

  Core Features:
  - TALLY MODE: Clean clicker counters with custom increment step sizes.
  - ROUNDS MODE: Cumulative score logging with rotating dealer/turn indicators. Perfect for Scrabble, Hearts, Rummy, and Uno.
  - VERSUS MODE (1v1): Tactile split touch panes with serve tracking, sets logging, and deuce (win by 2) validation. Great for Table Tennis, Darts, and Badminton.
  - STATS & ANALYTICS: Interactive score progression charts showing who was in the lead and when, along with match records.
  - 100% OFFLINE FUNCTIONAL: Installs instantly, loads in milliseconds, and works completely offline without ads. Keep scores in cabins, airplanes, or cafes.
  - UNDO ENGINE: Full global undo stack. Never lose a score progression by accident again.
  ```

---

## Step 5: Start Closed Testing (Personal Accounts)
If you registered a Personal Account after 2023, Google requires a 14-day testing period with at least 20 active testers before you can publish to Production:
1. Go to **Release** -> **Testing** -> **Closed testing**.
2. Create an **Active Track** (e.g., "Alpha Testing").
3. Add your testers: Create a Google Group or email list containing the Google Play Emails of **20 friends or family members**.
4. Create a release: Upload the `.aab` (Android App Bundle) generated by the packaging guide.
5. Copy the **Join on Android** link provided in the Play Console and share it with your 20 testers.
6. Once 20 testers have kept the app installed for **14 consecutive days**, click **Request Production Access** to launch on the global market!
